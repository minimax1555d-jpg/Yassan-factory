About Yassan

A factory built on two crafts, one standard

Yassan Furniture Factory works in two disciplines under one roof: solid wood joinery and structural metal fabrication. Most furniture makers choose one — we run both workshops side by side, so a single piece can move from the saw table to the welding bay without ever leaving our floor.

Every order, whether it's a hundred dining chairs for a restaurant or a custom steel-and-oak reception desk, passes through the same inspection line before it ships.

How a piece is made

Every order follows the same four stages, whether it starts as a plank of wood or a sheet of steel.

01

Material Selection

Timber and steel are graded and selected for the piece's specific load and finish requirements.

02

Cutting & Shaping

Wood is cut and joined; metal is cut, bent, and welded to frame specification.

03

Finishing

Staining and lacquer for wood; sanding and powder coating for metal surfaces.

04

Inspection & Delivery

Every piece is checked for stability and finish quality before it leaves the factory.
